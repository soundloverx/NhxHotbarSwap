# Changelog

## v1.0.0
- Hotbar Swap split out of NhxQualityPack, as a replacement for HotbarSwitch
- Favorited slots in the two rows (AzuEPI's, or NhxSimpleDeposit's own copy of them when AzuEPI isn't installed) are swapped along with their items, so every item stays exactly as protected as before. They're saved together with the character instead of on every swap
- Swapping no longer makes the game rebuild its known recipe and build piece lists, since only item positions change
- Won't load together with NhxQualityPack or HotbarSwitch
